Our story

It started with one shelf and a phone number

In 1998 our founder, Ramesh Iyer, took over a failing pharmacy in Chinna Thirupathi, Salem. The previous owner had sold half the shelf to make rent. Ramesh sold his scooter, restocked it from a distributor in the neighbouring state, and started keeping a written list of every customer who could not afford their medication.

Twenty-seven years later that list is a credit ledger, the scooter is parked outside MMR Complex, and the rule has not changed: if a customer cannot afford it, we find a way. We hold four generic alternatives for almost every brand we stock, and our pharmacists are trained to offer them without being asked twice.

How we grew, one decision at a time

No franchising, no investor, no acquisition offer accepted.

2004

Cold-chain capability added

Third-generation pharmacists join

Two of the counter staff are now pharmacy graduates, bringing clinical screening into the workflow.

2026

Still family owned in Chinna Thirupathi

45,000 SKUs, 22 pharmacists on duty, and the same affordability ledger in a shared system.

What has not changed

  • Every prescription is checked by a registered pharmacist, never by a robot.
  • Generic alternatives are offered the first time, not after you ask.
  • Expired stock is destroyed, not discounted and sold.
  • The founder still reads every adverse-event report personally.
  • We have never advertised a medicine on television.

A note on the ledger

Between 2009 and 2026 we have written off ₹11.4 lakh in medicine that patients could not pay for. It is recorded in the annual accounts, because we think you should be able to see it.

Quality

The five-point check before every parcel leaves

The same five checks run at every store, every shift, whether the order is one paracetamol strip or a twelve-item chronic refill.

01

Right patient

Name and date of birth matched against the label.

02

Right drug

Molecule, strength and formulation verified line by line.

03

Right dose

Checked against age, weight and diagnosis where known.

04

No interactions

Screened against the customer's own medicine history.

05

Right condition

Storage temperature logged, expiry checked, counselling note printed and handed over.

Left: the central cold locker. Right: sample preparation for compounding and paediatric suspensions.

Licensed and audited

Drug licence MC/SAL/1998/0042, GST registered, and inspected twice a year. Every cold-chain parcel ships with a temperature logger.

Compounding in-house

Paediatric suspensions, hormone preparations and dermatology creams compounded by our own pharmacist under a laminar-flow bench.

Returned & destroyed

Every expired pack is logged, photographed and destroyed with a vendor. Nothing is discounted and sold, not even at 90% off.

Open door policy

Ask any pharmacist to show you the audit register, the temperature log or the source invoice. We will show you.
