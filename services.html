How it works

Four steps, about twelve minutes

This is the standard flow for a prescription order. Most people complete it while making breakfast.

01

Send the prescription

Photograph it in our app or WhatsApp it. Blurry is fine — we ask for a clearer shot only if we must.

02

Pharmacist verifies

Legibility, expiry, dose, interactions and your allergy history. A reply lands within 20 minutes.

03

Confirm and pay

UPI, card or cash on delivery. Insurance and corporate rates are applied automatically.

04

Collect or receive

Walk in whenever suits you, or let the rider bring it to your door with a tracking link.

Service one

E-prescription upload

Send the prescription any way you like. We accept a photo, a PDF, a handwriting sample from a previous refill, or a plain message describing the drug name and dose to our pharmacy desk.

What we check before it leaves the counter

  • Prescriber name, registration number and signature are legible
  • No date has expired and every drug is available in stock
  • No interaction with the medicines already on your record
  • Dose matches your age, weight and kidney function where known
  • Nothing is substituted without a phone call to you first
